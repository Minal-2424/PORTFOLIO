A LITTLE CONTEXT

About the work.

I like understanding what happens beneath the surface: which services are exposed, what a packet reveals, and how a technical observation becomes a useful security finding.

I'm pursuing a B.Tech. in Computer Engineering at NMIMS. My cybersecurity practice has centred on authorised lab environments, network reconnaissance, traffic inspection, and documenting risks with practical mitigations.

I also work on research problems involving optimisation algorithms and experimental evaluation. That experience has shaped how I approach technical work: ask a clear question, test it methodically, and be precise about what the evidence supports.

I'm strengthening my foundations in Linux, networking, security tools, and cloud concepts while building a project record that shows my process-not just a list of technologies.

LSTM-Driven Adaptive MOEA/D for Multi-Criterion Optimization Problems

A prediction-guided approach to multi-objective optimisation that uses LSTM-based search-state prediction to adapt mutation probability within MOEA/D.

My contribution

Contributed to the approach, evaluated DTLZ and WFG benchmark suites, analysed results using six optimisation-quality indicators, and presented the paper.
